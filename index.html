Safety & Realism

1. Community Connection Protocol

BloodConnect serves strictly as a real-time communications network between emergency requesters and voluntary registered donors.

2. Professional Medical Screening

The application does NOT certify donor medical eligibility. The hospital/blood bank performs mandatory screening tests (hemoglobin, infectious disease markers, blood grouping) before collection.

3. Approximate Geolocation

Distances are displayed approximately (e.g. ~2.8 km) to maintain donor privacy and safety.

4. Opt-In Voluntarism

All emergency notifications are sent exclusively to donors who have actively toggled emergency opt-in settings.
